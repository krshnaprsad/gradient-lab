Blobs

Up to 24 colour points. Drag blobs on the canvas. Click one to change its colour. Double-click one to remove it.

Blend

Sweep uses the first four blob colours as field, band, lobe and corner glow. Fold X/Y moves the tip, Silk fold bends the lobe, Swirl tilts the band and Softness softens its edges. Extra blobs add local colour accents.

Flow layers four broad colour shapes. Drag their blobs to move each shape. Fold X/Y moves the composition, Silk fold bends the cyan seam, Swirl tilts the shapes and Softness changes their edges. Edge fade blends into the background.
